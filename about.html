Who we are and why we search

Who We Are

The Find Grandiddy Society (FGS) is a dedicated group of friends who came together in November 2026 after Grandiddy Connor was last seen at the Minerals Hotel in Vernon, NJ. We have one goal: bring Grandiddy home.

Our Mission

We will not rest until Grandiddy is found. We collect sightings, follow every lead, and keep his legend alive. Wherever there is a Toys-R-Us, a midnight elementary school, or a hallway in need of detention, the Society is watching.

What We Stand For

  • Loyalty: Once a friend of Grandiddy, always a friend of Grandiddy.
  • Persistence: No lead is too small, no hallway too long.
  • Community: It takes a village to find a 6'9" man.

How to Join the Society

  • Share this website with everyone you know.
  • Post with #savegrandiddy.
  • Report any Grandiddy sightings to the Society.
