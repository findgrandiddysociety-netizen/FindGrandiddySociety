THE LEGEND OF GRANDIDDY

The history of Grandiddy Connor

Before he vanished, Grandiddy Connor was a towering figure in his community, literally. At 6'9" with long, black, curly hair and his trademark glasses, he was impossible to miss. This is his story so far.

The Early Years

A Legend Is Born

Grandiddy entered the world and immediately began growing. He never really stopped.

The Growth Spurt

Reaching 6'9"

Somewhere along the way, Grandiddy reached a height of 6'9" and grew the long, curly hair that would become his signature look.

The Toys-R-Us Era

Dancing Into History

Grandiddy became known for twerking at the Toys-R-Us, a talent that earned him local fame.

The Midnight Walks

Harrison Elementary After Dark

For reasons no one fully understands, Grandiddy took regular midnight walks at Harrison Elementary School.

The Detention Years

A Record-Breaking Record

Grandiddy became a regular in five-hour detentions for PDA in the halls. The detention room was practically his second home.

November 11

The Disappearance

Grandiddy was last seen at the Minerals Hotel in Vernon, NJ. He has not been seen since.

Today

The Society Forms

The Find Grandiddy Society was founded to bring him home. The search continues.
